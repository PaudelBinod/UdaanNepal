About the organizer

About Udaan Nepal

Udaan Nepal Pvt. Ltd. organizes UNSMP as part of its work supporting Nepali students with practical, accessible guidance and education. This seminar is one part of a broader effort to help students approach their next steps with more confidence and clarity.
