Who we are

Education built for the Nepali classroom, not imported from somewhere else.

Udaan Nepal Pvt. Ltd. is a private educational institution founded to make quality learning affordable and practical for Nepali students — combining online courses, digital tools, and a purpose-built studio for modern teaching.

A

Locally grounded

Curriculum and teaching methods designed around the real Nepali classroom, not translated foreign material.

B

Affordable by design

Pricing structured so quality education stays within reach of everyday Nepali families.

C

Built by practitioners

Founded and run by engineers and teachers — people who've sat in the same classrooms our students sit in.
