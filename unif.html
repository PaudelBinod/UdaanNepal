01 — About UNIF

What is UNIF?

Udaan Nepal Innovation Fair (UNIF) is the national platform designed to foster scientific curiosity, technological ingenuity, and entrepreneurial spirit among students and young innovators across Nepal.

🔭

Our Vision

Encourage young people to imagine and build solutions for the future.

🚀

Our Mission

Provide a platform where innovative ideas can be developed, demonstrated, and recognized.

🤝

Our Purpose

Connect students, innovators, educators, organizations, and communities through innovation.
